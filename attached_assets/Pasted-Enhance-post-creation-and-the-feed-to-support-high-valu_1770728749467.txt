Enhance post creation and the feed to support high-value and structured barter items like real estate (houses/apartments), vehicles (cars), yachts, luxury watches, and other assets, while keeping everyday trades simple.

Key changes:

1. **Category System Upgrade**
   - Expand categories with sub-types:
     - Real Estate (House, Apartment, Villa, Office Space, Commercial)
     - Vehicles (Car, Motorcycle, Yacht/Boat, Truck/Van)
     - Luxury Goods (Watches, Jewelry, Art, Electronics)
     - Services & Skills, Food & Hospitality, Space & Office, Other (existing)

2. **Smart Post Creation Form**
   - When user selects a category, dynamically show relevant structured fields (use conditional rendering).
   - All posts must still include: Title, Description, Photos/Video (multiple), "What I Offer" summary, "What I Want in Return", Declared AED Value (mandatory, with note: "Use professional appraisal for high-value items")
   - Add optional "Market Valuation / Trends" section: text field for user to paste/link current market value, trends, or forecast (e.g., "Dubizzle average AED 2.5M for similar villas")

3. **Category-Specific Fields**

   - **Real Estate**:
     - Property Type (dropdown)
     - Bedrooms, Bathrooms (numbers)
     - Square Meters / Sq Ft
     - Year Built
     - Location (city, area, Google Maps link optional)
     - Amenities (multi-select: Pool, Gym, Parking, Sea View, Furnished, Balcony, Garden, Security, etc.)
     - Title Deed / Ownership Status
     - Additional: Floor number, View type

   - **Vehicles (Cars / Yachts / Boats)**:
     - Make, Model, Year
     - Mileage / Hours Used
     - Number of Doors / Cabins
     - Engine Type (e.g., Petrol, Diesel, Electric, Hybrid) + Capacity
     - Transmission (Auto/Manual)
     - Condition (New/Used, Excellent/Good/Fair)
     - Features/Amenities (multi-select: Leather Seats, Navigation, Sunroof, AC, Sound System, etc.)
     - Registration/Insurance Expiry
     - Color, Fuel Efficiency

   - **Luxury Watches / Goods**:
     - Brand, Model
     - Year, Condition
     - Material (Gold, Steel, etc.)
     - Features (Chronograph, Waterproof, etc.)
     - Box & Papers Included? (yes/no)
     - Serial Number (optional)

   - For "Need/Request" posts: Mirror the same fields so users can specify exactly what they're looking for (e.g., "Want 3-bedroom villa with pool in Jumeirah")

4. **Feed & Post Display**
   - In the feed card, prominently show key structured details (e.g., "2022 Toyota Camry • 4 Doors • 35k km • AED 85k value")
   - For real estate: Show bedrooms/bathrooms/sqm icons
   - Make cards richer with badges (e.g., "High Value", "Verified Owner")

5. **Validation & UX**
   - Required fields per category (e.g., bedrooms for real estate)
   - Help tooltips: "Accurate details build trust and better matches"
   - Keep form mobile-friendly and fast

6. **Seed Data**
   - Add 10 new realistic high-value fake posts: 4 real estate (Dubai villas/apartments), 3 cars, 2 yachts, 1 luxury watch — with full structured details filled.

After this, post creation will feel professional for big-ticket items while staying simple for everyday trades.
