## Voiceover Script (clean read — English)

> Across the UAE, businesses are sitting on unused capacity —
> empty tables, idle inventory, spare hours.
>
> What if you could trade what you have… for what you actually need?
>
> Meet Bareter — the UAE's cashless marketplace where businesses barter, not buy.
>
> Step one: list what you offer — a service, a product, anything of value.
>
> Step two: match with verified businesses, vetted by Didit KYC.
>
> Step three: negotiate, sign a binding contract, and deliver — all in one place.
>
> More liquidity. A stronger network. Zero cash exchanged.
>
> Bareter. Trade smarter. Join the waitlist at bareter.com.

**Word count:** ~95 words → reads in ~58s at a calm 95 wpm pace, leaving 2s of breathing room at the open and close.

**Recommended VO:** Warm, mid-pitch, confident male or female voice with neutral international English (or Emirati-tinged English for stronger local resonance). Suggested talents on **ElevenLabs**: *Adam (calm)*, *Charlotte (warm)*, or *Daniel (corporate)*.

## Editing Notes

1. **Color grade:** Push every clip toward a unified warm-teal palette to match brand. Lift shadows slightly, add a touch of golden-hour warmth in highlights.
2. **Typography:** Use the same font stack as bareter.com (Inter, semibold for step numbers, regular for body). Step numbers in deep teal `#0F766E` on a soft cream chip.
3. **Transitions:** Use only soft cross-dissolves (0.4s) between clips. No flashy wipes.
4. **Captions:** Burn-in English captions for silent autoplay (Instagram/LinkedIn feed). Place in lower third with 8% black scrim.
5. **Versions to export from this master:**
   - `bareter-explainer-60s-1080p.mp4` (16:9, hero — website + YouTube)
